# Notifications

Notifications tell you when something needs your attention so you don't have to watch the screen — a session finished, hit an error, asked a question, or needs permission to do something. Set them up at **Settings → OpenChamber → Notifications**.

## Turn them on

1. Open **Settings → OpenChamber → Notifications**.
2. Allow notifications when your browser or system asks.
3. Choose what you want to be told about:
   - a session **finishes**
   - a session hits an **error**
   - a session **asks a question**
   - a session needs **permission**
   - **subtasks** complete

## How they reach you

- on **desktop**, you get native system notifications
- in a **browser or installed app**, you get web push notifications, so they arrive even when the tab is in the background
- in the **iPhone and Android apps**, you get push notifications. What they say is encrypted between your server and your phone, so the push services on the way see only a generic title. On your phone each one shows the session name and what happened: the agent's reply, its question, or the command it wants to run
- through an **extension**, such as one that posts to Slack or Matrix: once you allow the extension and connect its account in [Settings → Integrations](/integrations/#extension-notification-channels), it gets the same notifications your phone would, following the toggles above, while no window is visible. Pause the extension to stop them

Sessions set to auto-accept won't nag you with permission notifications.

## The notification center

The bell in the header lists every notification the server raised in the last 7 days, the newest first, including the ones that arrived while you were in the app. A dot marks the unread ones, and the bell shows how many there are. Opening a session marks its notifications read on every device. Click one to jump to its session, or filter to the unread ones and the ones waiting for you: questions, permissions and errors.

The center also keeps what happened while you looked elsewhere: an OpenCode restart that cut off running chats (open the entry to see each chat), a fix loop that finished or waits for your approval, a stacked-PR restack stopped on a conflict, a failed scheduled task, and OpenChamber and OpenCode updates. **Needs you** shows what still waits for you, read or not: a question or permission until you answer it, a restart until every chat is continued or dismissed, a fix loop until you approve it, a restack until it goes on, and an update until it is installed. Errors stay there until you read them. The list cleans itself: a finished turn moves to **History** once you have seen it or written in that chat again, and a question or permission moves there once it is answered. Anything older than 7 days, or past the newest 200, is deleted. Several finished turns of one chat show as one row, and the button at the end of a row removes it.

With a [classification provider](/classification-providers/) set up, Jev also reads each finished turn. A turn that looks done or has nothing to act on lands in the notification center without an alert or push; the others alert as before, a few seconds later. Questions, permissions and errors never go through Jev. Turn it off with **Let Jev decide which finished turns alert you**.

## Customize the wording

Each kind of notification has a title and message template you can edit, using fields like the agent name and model. There's also a limit on how much of the last message is included, so notifications stay short.
