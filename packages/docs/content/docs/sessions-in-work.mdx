# Sessions in Work

The **In work** block at the top of the sidebar holds the sessions you're working in until you mark them done, however long ago you last opened them. Your projects keep every session you've ever had; In work holds only what you haven't finished.

## Where to find it

The **In work** block sits in the sidebar under Chats, above your projects or the list by time. A work in it leaves its project, so each work shows once. To keep it under its project too, open **Customize sections…** in the sidebar's display menu and turn on **Also in projects** on the In work row. Search lists it once, in the In work block.

Each row takes one line: the title and its status, with a coloured mark when it has a pull request or issue. Rows are ordered by latest activity, so they show no date. Hover a row to see its project, branch, time and links, or turn on **Detailed rows** in the display menu to show them under the title. Detailed rows apply under your projects too; the sessions inside a work stay on one line.

Pinned sessions have their own **Pinned** block above In work; to keep them only under their projects, turn off the Pinned switch in **Customize sections…**. A pinned session in work is listed in Pinned only.

The phone's sessions list is the same. Its display menu has **Also show in work under projects** as an item of its own. Its rows take two lines unless you turn off **Detailed rows**, and the arrow that opens a work is always there, since there is nothing to hover.

## How sessions get in

Track a session by hand, as described below, or let sessions go in on their own with **Sessions enter In work** in **Settings → Sessions → Sessions in work**:

- **Off**, the default: only sessions you track go in.
- **Every message:** send a message in a session and it goes in. A session you marked done comes back when you send a new message in it.
- **Jev decides:** with a [classification provider](/classification-providers/) set up, Jev reads each message you send and moves the session in when you ask for a change, report a bug or discuss a concrete change you're about to make. Questions, explanations and research don't count. Jev looks again when the agent finishes a turn, which catches work that only becomes clear from the reply. A session you marked done comes back only when Jev sees new work in a message sent after that. Without a classification provider, Jev decides works like Off.

This covers any project session, including sessions in worktrees.

If you had switched **Show sessions in work** on yourself before this setting arrived, it starts at **Every message**, which is how sessions got in until then. Otherwise it starts at Off.

Chats, archived sessions, review sessions and runs of [scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks/) never go in on their own; fork a run to keep working on it, and the fork does. A subagent never puts its parent in work.

## Task names

The agent names each work after its task as soon as the task is clear, in a few words such as "Login timeout fix". A worktree keeps the name in its branch description, so you see it everywhere you open the repository; a session in the project folder takes the name as its title. Rename it yourself and your name stays: the agent never renames a task you named.

## Track and finish a session by hand

- **Track:** hover a row and press the eye, or choose **Track as in work** in the session menu.
- **Finish:** hover a row in the block and press the check, or choose **Mark done** in the menu. Done finishes the whole work: every session in it that was still in work.

Done on a worktree takes it out of In work and leaves it where it is. To also archive it when you mark it done, turn on **Archive a worktree when you mark it done** in **Settings → Sessions**: the folder goes, and its sessions, changes and branch wait on the Archive page, ready to restore. If a session there is still running, the worktree stays.

On the phone, swipe a row to the side to reach the same action.

Chats don't get the eye: they're plain conversations.

### "This work looks done"

With a [classification provider](/classification-providers/) set up, Jev checks each turn of a session in work. When a turn looks like the end of the work (the agent reports finished work, isn't going on by itself, isn't waiting for your answer and offers nothing more), Jev leaves a hint instead of closing anything:

- a grey check on the session row in the sidebar
- a strip above the composer: **This work looks done.** with a **Mark done** button

The hint shows once you've been quiet for 10 minutes after the turn, so it stays out of the way while you move from one step to the next. Press **Mark done** if Jev is right. If not, just keep going: the hint disappears with your next message. Without a classification provider there's no hint, and you mark sessions done yourself.

## Recap and next-message suggestion

After the agent finishes and the session stays quiet for a minute, OpenChamber can write two things with your Small Model (see [Providers, Models & Agents](/providers/)):

- **Recap:** a short reminder of what was done and where things stand, shown under the last reply and in the session's tooltip in the sidebar
- **Suggestion:** the next message you'd probably send, as a row at the top of the composer; click it to put the text in the composer, it never sends by itself

With Jev available, OpenChamber first asks it whether the turn needs them. A plain "thanks, that's it" after real work still gets a recap, but a suggestion is skipped when nothing is left to do, and the Small Model isn't called at all when neither is useful. Without Jev both are generated after every turn, as before.

Turn them on or off in **Settings → Chat → Session Assistance**.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Sessions → Sessions in work**:

- **Show sessions in work**, on by default: turn it off and the whole feature is off, with no block and no session going in on its own.
- **Sessions enter In work**: Off, Every message or Jev decides, as described in [How sessions get in](#how-sessions-get-in).

Session cleanup keeps sessions in the In work block. To let them expire like any other session, turn on **Include sessions in In work** under **Settings → Sessions → Session retention**. It's off by default, and pinned sessions stay either way.

## Where it works

The block and the manual track/done work everywhere, VS Code included. Sessions going in on their own, the "looks done" hint and the Jev filter for recaps need the OpenChamber server, so they work in the desktop app, the web app and on your phone, but not in VS Code. In VS Code you track sessions by hand.
