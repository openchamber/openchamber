# Sessions in Work

Every session you send a message in goes into the **In work** block at the top of the sidebar and stays there until you mark it done, however long ago you last opened it. Your projects keep every session you've ever had; In work holds only what you haven't finished.

## Where to find it

The **In work** block sits in the sidebar under Chats, above your projects or the timeline. On the phone it's in the same place in the sessions list.

A session in work also stays in its project and folder, and under its project in the timeline, so you see it in two places. Its subsessions come along with it. Search lists it once, in the In work block.

## How sessions get in

Send a message in a session and it goes into In work. This covers any project session, including sessions in worktrees.

A session you marked done comes back when you send a new message in it.

Chats, archived sessions, review sessions and runs of [scheduled tasks](/scheduled-tasks/) never go in; fork a run to keep working on it, and the fork does. Subsessions don't go in on their own; they come with their parent.

## Track and finish a session by hand

- **Track:** hover a session and press the eye, or choose **Track as in work** in the session menu.
- **Finish:** hover a session in the block and press the check, or choose **Mark done** in the menu.

On the phone, swipe a session row to reach the same action, or use the session menu.

Chats don't get the eye: they're plain conversations.

### "This work looks done"

With a [classification provider](/classification-providers/) set up, Jev checks each turn of a session in work. When a turn looks like the end of the work (you said thanks, asked for a commit, confirmed it works), Jev leaves a hint instead of closing anything:

- a grey check on the session row in the sidebar
- a strip above the composer: **This work looks done.** with a **Mark done** button

Press **Mark done** if Jev is right. If not, just keep going: the hint disappears with your next message. Without a classification provider there's no hint, and you mark sessions done yourself.

## Recap and next-message suggestion

After the agent finishes and the session stays quiet for a minute, OpenChamber can write two things with your Small Model (see [Providers, Models & Agents](/providers/)):

- **Recap:** a short reminder of what was done and where things stand, shown under the last reply and in the session's tooltip in the sidebar
- **Suggestion:** the next message you'd probably send, as a row at the top of the composer; click it to put the text in the composer, it never sends by itself

With Jev available, OpenChamber first asks it whether the turn needs them. A plain "thanks, that's it" after real work still gets a recap, but a suggestion is skipped when nothing is left to do, and the Small Model isn't called at all when neither is useful. Without Jev both are generated after every turn, as before.

Turn them on or off in **Settings → Chat → Session Assistance**.

## Settings

Open **Settings → Sessions → Sessions in work**. There's one setting, **Show sessions in work**, on by default. Turn it off and the whole feature is off: no block, and sending a message no longer puts sessions in work.

To hide only the block, use **Show in work section** in the sidebar's display menu.

## Where it works

The block and the manual track/done work everywhere, VS Code included. Tracking sessions when you send a message, the "looks done" hint and the Jev filter for recaps need the OpenChamber server, so they work in the desktop app, the web app and on your phone, but not in VS Code. In VS Code you track sessions by hand.
